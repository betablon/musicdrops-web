2. Overview

MusicDrops is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. The app does not require user accounts. Your data stays on your device (with optional iCloud sync, see Section 5).

3. Data We Do Not Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not do:

  • We do not collect personal information (names, email addresses, etc.)
  • We do not track your usage or behaviour
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not set cookies ourselves. Our CDN provider (Cloudflare) may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect against malicious traffic (see Section 12)
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes (for necessary data exchanges with Apple’s services, see Section 5)
  • We do not require or offer user accounts
  • We do not collect your data to build profiles about you, share it for profiling by third parties, or make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR)

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Your list of followed artists and your in-app curation choices
  • Cached release information and artwork
  • Your notification and filter preferences
  • Your subscription status
  • A random, per-device identifier stored in the iOS Keychain, used solely to coordinate local release notifications across your own devices when iCloud sync is enabled. This identifier is generated on your device, never transmitted to our servers, and not accessible to us or to any third party.
  • Data derived from your Apple Music library and listening history, retained on your device only if you choose to use a feature that reads it (for example follow suggestions from “Import from Apple Music”, or listening recaps; see Section 5). Depending on the feature, this may include artist names, artwork references, per-artist and per-release rankings, and play statistics. This data stays on your device, and you can clear an imported snapshot at any time from within MusicDrops.

This data is stored solely for the purpose of providing the app’s core functionality. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / provision of the service you requested), except for the Apple Music library snapshot, which is based on your explicit consent (see Section 5).

5. Third-Party Services

MusicDrops communicates with the following Apple services to provide its functionality. When the app communicates with these services, certain technical data (such as your IP address) may be transmitted to Apple’s servers.

Apple Music API

We use Apple’s MusicKit / Apple Music API to search for artists and fetch release information (albums, singles, EPs). These requests go directly from your device to Apple’s servers. We do not operate any intermediary servers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary for providing the service).

Apple Music Library & Listening Data (Optional)

If you choose to use features that draw on your Apple Music library and listening history, such as importing artists to suggest who to follow, or in-app listening recaps and statistics, MusicDrops reads data from your Apple Music library on your device (such as artist names, play counts, and last-played dates) through Apple’s MusicKit framework. This processing happens entirely on your device; the underlying library and listening data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to our servers. MusicDrops retains on your device only the data a feature you use needs, for example a per-artist ranking or play statistics (see Section 4). Features that personalise what you see — for example ranking or highlighting releases based on how often you listen to them — run entirely on your device, use only data you have consented to share with the App, and can be turned off at any time. You can grant or revoke Apple Music access at any time from iOS Settings, and clear any imported snapshot at any time from within MusicDrops.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, given by enabling Apple Music access and initiating the import). You can withdraw consent at any time as described above; the withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)

Push notifications for new releases are delivered through Apple’s Push Notification service. Apple generates a device token to deliver notifications. We do not use any third-party push notification providers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, which you provide by enabling notifications in your device settings). You can revoke this consent at any time by disabling notifications for MusicDrops in your device settings.

iCloud / CloudKit

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, the App uses Apple’s CloudKit to sync your followed artists, your in-app curation choices (such as suggested artists you have hidden), and related app data across your Apple devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is only accessible to you. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary for providing the service).

iTunes Store RSS (Album Reviews)

The App fetches album reviews from Apple’s iTunes Store RSS feeds for the countries you have enabled in your review source settings. These requests go directly from your device to Apple’s servers. We do not operate any intermediary servers and do not store reviews outside your device.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary for providing the service).

App Store / StoreKit

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely through Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). All payment processing is managed by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

Apple Translation (On-Device)

The App offers an option to translate album reviews using Apple’s on-device Translation framework. Translations are performed entirely on your device. However, the framework may download language packs from Apple’s servers if they are not already installed. No review content is sent to Apple for translation.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary for providing the service).

For details on how Apple processes your data, please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): the right to obtain information about whether and how your personal data is processed.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): the right to have your personal data deleted.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): the right to request that processing of your data be restricted.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): the right to object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Right related to automated decision-making (Art. 22 GDPR): the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing.

You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR) where processing is based on consent. The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

Since MusicDrops does not collect or store any personal data on our servers, these rights are satisfied by design. All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Apple’s services, please contact Apple directly or refer to their privacy policy.

To exercise any of these rights or if you have questions, please contact us at hello@musicdrops.app.
